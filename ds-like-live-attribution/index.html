How these numbers are counted
  • EverWebinar DEAL SCOUT LIKE-LIVE (#54) for 2026: the October 14, October 21, and October 30 sessions, plus the direct-link room.
  • A person is one email address. The registration total counts every signup, so the same person can be in more than one session.
  • Attended means WebinarJam marked them present live or on the replay.
  • A purchase is a Deal Scout Close lead moved to Signed Up within 7 days after that view, matched on email. It is credited to the session they watched most recently before they bought.
  • Minutes are the time WebinarJam recorded. The watch-time chart uses each attendee’s total across their views.
  • Channel comes from the registration UTM. Email is medium = email. No UTM is a blank source and medium. Google ads is source google or DG. The rest of this account is Meta: Facebook, Instagram, Audience Network, and Threads.
  • Campaign is utm_campaign. Ad creative is utm_content. Blank tags show as (none).
  • Counted November 9, 2026. This page is a snapshot, not a live feed.
