# Top Tier webinar attribution

TC LIKE-LIVE registrations compared with Top Tier Close leads who moved to
Signed Up within 7 days of watching. Snapshot of November 9, 2026.

## Open it

Double-click `index.html`. One file, no build step, no server, no login, and
no network calls. Names, emails, and phone numbers are not in the page.

The repo is private. GitHub's file view will not run the page, and publishing
it with GitHub Pages would put the ad results on the public internet. Open the
file locally, or share the repo with the people who should see it.

## What you can do on the page

- Switch light, dark, or auto.
- Read each session's registrations, attendance, and purchases.
- Compare channel, campaign, or ad creative, then sort and limit the rows.
- Filter the buyer list. Buyers are numbered in purchase order.

## How a purchase is counted

EverWebinar #49, every signup from May 19 through October 28, 2026, plus the
direct-link room. Attended means WebinarJam marked them live or replay. A
purchase is a Top Tier lead moved to Signed Up within 7 days after that view,
matched on email, and credited to the session they watched most recently
before they bought.
